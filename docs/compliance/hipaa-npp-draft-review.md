# Corazón Salud NPP review draft

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL/COMPLIANCE REVIEW — NOT AN ADOPTED NOTICE**

This is a review outline, not a notice effective for patients. Do not publish it as adopted, invent an effective date or replace the website/portal privacy policy. Patient Agreement v5 remains unchanged.

## Required decisions before a publishable draft

- Determine whether EMINAT Medical Center, LLC is a HIPAA covered provider for the relevant activities, based on standard electronic transactions, including through intermediaries. A membership program alone does not settle that question.
- Obtain the existing adopted EMC notice, if any, and evidence of its delivery, acknowledgement and revision procedures.
- Confirm every entity and location governed by the notice, the privacy official/contact title and telephone, request channels and complaint process.
- Confirm actual permitted practices and more protective state laws; do not promise services or sharing practices the organization does not use.
- Determine whether EMC receives or maintains records subject to 42 CFR Part 2, as well as whether it is itself a Part 2 program. These are distinct questions.

## Conditional Part 2 review

Add and approve applicable Part 2 restrictions only after applicability and flows are confirmed. Review limits on use/disclosure of records or testimony in proceedings against the patient and fundraising choices where applicable. Do not present ordinary household billing authority as authorization to view another adult's medical record.

The HHS provider model was reviewed February 13, 2026. Applicable surviving NPP changes had a February 16, 2026 compliance date. HHS states that the June 18, 2025 judgment vacated most of the 2024 reproductive-health rule, including 164.520(b)(1)(ii)(F), (G), (H). Do not copy those vacated provisions as current mandatory requirements.
